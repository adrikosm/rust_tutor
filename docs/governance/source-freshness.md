# Quarterly source freshness review

Owner: product owner; technical review may be delegated but the release decision may not.

Trigger: the first working day of January, April, July, and October; also before a release when a compiler/library/source snapshot is older than 90 days or a pinned dependency/toolchain changes.

Review URLs, upstream versions/commits, Rust/compiler behavior, maintenance status, license/use decisions, content claims, and golden fixtures. Network failure or changed content marks the source `stale_pending_review`; it does not silently delete history. Stale sources may remain historical evidence, but block new or changed released claims that depend on them until reverified or superseded.
