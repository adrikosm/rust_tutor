# Canonical product glossary

Each term has one product meaning. More specific schemas may add fields but cannot redefine it.

| Term | Canonical definition |
|---|---|
| concept | A stable, teachable unit of knowledge or reasoning with a reviewed scope. |
| outcome | An observable behavior that can be independently assessed and linked to evidence. |
| item | A versioned learner-facing prompt, exercise, check, assessment, or project task. |
| attempt | One presentation of one immutable item version to a learner. |
| event | An immutable, ordered fact recorded during an attempt or product workflow. |
| evidence | A versioned observation derived from identified events or artifacts against an outcome rubric. |
| mastery | A rebuildable, model-versioned claim supported by independent, delayed, and transfer evidence—not completion or confidence. |
| review | A scheduled or performed retrieval opportunity whose timing and evidence remain explicit. |
| transfer | Successful application of an outcome to a sufficiently changed context without disallowed support. |
